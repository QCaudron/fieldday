[![Civil Defense Poster](../assets/images/srfd_CD.png)](../assets/images/srfd_CD.png)

CQ CQ CQ! Calling all operators!

Seattle Radio Field Day 2026 is just around the corner. The Puget Sound Repeater Group, West Seattle Amateur Radio Club, Seattle Auxiliary Communications Service, and Cascadia Radio are teaming up for a massive joint operation, and we have a place for you and your third party traffic.

Whether you're an Extra class veteran, a newly-minted Technician, or an unlicensed enthusiast looking to make your very first contact, there's a seat at the operating table for you. We'll be running HF, VHF, and UHF on voice, CW, and digital modes. Get On The Air (GOTA) Stations will be set up and ready to go, with experienced mentors on standby to help you break through the noise floor or try out a brand new mode.

Beyond contesting, this weekend is about sharpening our skills as a national resource. We'll be operating 100% on emergency power using nothing but batteries and solar, as well as hosting educational workshops throughout the event.

📍 **Where:** South Seattle College, North Field  
📅 **When:** June 27 & 28  
💵 **Cost:** 100% Free!  
📻 **On-Site Simplex:** 147.520 MHz  

Check out [SeattleRadioFieldDay.org](https://www.seattleradiofieldday.org/) for the full schedule and to let us know you're coming. Come hang out, make new friends, explore the off-grid tech, and get on the air.

We hope to see you in the logs! 73!
